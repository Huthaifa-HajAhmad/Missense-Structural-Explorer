Method: `src/shared/evidenceTabs.ts` (the one tab list) → `src/components/EvidenceDashboardCard.tsx:237-246` (tab dispatch) → `src/components/inspector/EvidenceInspector.tsx:RAILS` (rail map) → the tab component and its imports.
