3. **Insights and Report understate how ClinVar affects the tier.** Both now show the separate assertion, condition and source, and correctly describe retirement of PP5/BP6 (`src/components/insights/InsightsScreen.tsx:37`, `src/shared/reportDocument.ts:102`). 5. **PM1 still depends on when the export is made.** The requested four callers carry the boolean. The Report hook converts a missing, loading or failed hotspot read to false (`src/components/report/useReportDocument.ts:48`, `src/shared/reportInput.ts:61`, `src/stores/slices/searchSlice.ts:393`, `src/components/inspector/InsightsInspector.tsx:61`). Withhold unverified entries from native totals and scoring while keeping successfully checked entries available.
